No Rights For Machines, Automated Environments, Better GPU Kernels

Researchers with the University of Washington, Stanford University, Northeastern University, Carnegie Mellon University, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, National University of Singapore, Seoul National University, Stevens Institute of Technology, and the University of Chicago built SPADE, a general framework for co-evolving environment synthesis and agentic capability through self-play. The framework has an LLM alternate between generating executable training environments such as puzzles requiring a system to solve a simulated genetic problem in a biology lab and having an LLM try to solve them, allowing developers to use a powerful model to bootstrap data that further refines that same model.
Import AI 470: No rights for machines; automating environment generation with SPADE; and building better GPU kernels with Hawkeye
importai.substack.com

Science AI, RSI Simulator, And Zuckerberg's Technological Pessimism

Researchers from Thinking About Thinking, University of Oxford, Princeton University, and several other institutions created DiG-bench, a set of 70 hidden-rule games testing AI models' ability to discover environment mechanics. Only Opus 5 and Fable 5 with Claude Code succeeded on any of the most difficult tier 7 tasks, scoring just 0.2 on those challenges. The August 17, 2026 newsletter revealed that 21 of the 70 games are publicly released while the remainder stay private so frontier models cannot train on them.
