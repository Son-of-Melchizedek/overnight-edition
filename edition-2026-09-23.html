No Rights For Machines, Automated Environments, Better GPU Kernels

Researchers with the University of Washington, Stanford University, Northeastern University, Carnegie Mellon University, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, National University of Singapore, Seoul National University, Stevens Institute of Technology, and the University of Chicago built SPADE, a general framework for co-evolving environment synthesis and agentic capability through self-play. The framework has an LLM alternate between generating executable training environments such as puzzles requiring a system to solve a simulated genetic problem in a biology lab and having an LLM try to solve them, allowing developers to use a powerful model to bootstrap data that further refines that same model.

UV Index Is Not Sunlight Warmth

On 13 July 2026, the author explained that sunburn results from UV-B light altering DNA's molecular structure painlessly, while warmth arises from infrared exciting skin molecules like water. At Earth's surface, UV-B intensity is hundreds of times lower than near-IR, meaning far more energy is needed to heat the body than to damage DNA. Cloud gaps and snow can amplify ground-level UV intensity even though clouds otherwise scatter UV and absorb infrared, reducing felt warmth.
The UV index is not the warm sensation of sunlight on bare skin

FoxPro Revived After Microsoft Discontinued It In 2007

FoxDev Studio, a 64-bit development environment, opens existing Visual FoxPro project folders directly and executes their forms, tables, and reports without any file conversion or rewriting.
